How ReelForge works, and how to get the best reels.

How does a reel get made?

ReelForge opens both websites in a real browser, dismisses cookie banners and popups, forces lazy content to load, detects the visually interesting sections (hero, cards, testimonials, CTAs), then films a smooth scripted tour — glide to a section, settle, dwell while the camera pushes in, glide on. The intro card, labels, transition, end card and music bed are composed around the footage and rendered to MP4.

Connecting Facebook (5 minutes)

1. Create a Business app at developers.facebook.com.
2. In Graph API Explorer grant pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement.
3. Extend the token, call /me/accounts, and copy your Page’s token.
4. Set FB_PAGE_ID and FB_PAGE_ACCESS_TOKEN in the server’s environment variables and redeploy. The Post button then publishes directly to your Page. For client-owned pages, route through the Make.com webhook instead.

Why does my export have a watermark?

Free-plan exports include a small watermark. Starter and above export clean. Your current plan is on the Plan & usage page.

A site captured badly — what do I do?

Check the before/after thumbnails on the project page first. Sites behind aggressive bot protection (Cloudflare challenges) may refuse automated visits. Popups that re-open constantly, or WebGL-heavy pages on small servers, occasionally fall back to “lite mode” (no 3D). Re-running the project usually helps; if a specific site consistently fails, send it to support — every hostile site we fix makes the engine better.
